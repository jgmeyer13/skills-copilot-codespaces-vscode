The Relay a rotating dual-pivot attack

Two playmakers — the fly-half (10) and fullback (15) — alternate as the ON pivot (first receiver running the play) and the OFF pivot (standing at depth, reading the field and calling the next play like a QB scanning coverage). The scrum-half (9) is the constant link. After every ruck the pivots cross over: the one who just passed drops to the backfield as a safety; the deep one steps up to attack.
