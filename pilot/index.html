    Are the progress shots comparable?

    The question keep rate cannot answer. Measured on the frame actually captured, against the frame it will actually be compared with. Returning members only — a first visit had nothing to line up against.

    The rig — did the photographs line themselves up?

    Only returning members can be registered at all, so first visits are not counted as failures here. The refusals are the half worth reading: a pile of them for the same reason is something physical going wrong in the room.

    Has the light moved?

    Against the reading taken when the rig was saved, in stops. A tenth of a stop is invisible; a whole one is a different photograph. Sessions on a different camera are left out — a different sensor reading the same wall is a different number.
