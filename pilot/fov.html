How to measure · 5 minutes

  1. Tape a tape measure vertically up a wall, zero on the floor. Or tape strips every 100mm and number them.
  2. Stand the camera on something solid, lens exactly square to the wall — not tilted up or down. Tilt is the one error that ruins this.
  3. Measure the horizontal distance from the lens to the wall and type it in. Anything from 1000mm up works; the maths scales.
  4. Read the highest and lowest marks you can see on the blue centre line, and type those in.
